Vascular atlas

Three-dimensional visualizations of the heart, neck and brain vasculature. Every scene draws on the SPL Head & Neck Atlas, the JHU parcellation and BrainCharter data; the space is RAS, in mm. The info boxes note that the scan is cut at z ≈ −160 mm and that the JHU alignment is approximate (scale 0.865, rotation 3.6°, median residual 1.2 mm).

General scenes

Scenes that traverse the whole pathway or show several systems together.

What is the connection of heart, neck and brain?animasyon↗

The full tour of the vessels running from the heart up the neck to the skull base: starting at the aortic arch, labeled with JHU regions. You can adjust the opacity of the bone, vessel and soft-tissue layers with sliders; it also works in VR on Quest via WebXR, and a PWA version is available for offline use in flight.

3dtry.github.io/animasyon

Skull, vessels and brain, layer by layervessels↗

The aggregate version, with the skull, vessels and brain parcellation stacked layer by layer. Switching the layers on and off one by one lets you examine the same pathway from different cuts.

3dtry.github.io/vessels

Walk through the JHU brain regions in 3DJHUvr↗

A virtual-reality scene showing the JHU regions (ROIs) in 3D. It also runs in the browser without a headset; you can rotate and explode the regions to inspect each one on its own.

3dtry.github.io/JHUvr/jhu-vr.html

Arteries and veins as one connected networkbraincharter_arterial_venous_connected↗

The BrainCharter arterial and venous trees shown together as connected networks, with every named vessel listed in the legend beside its length. Arteries and veins can be shown alone or side by side, one-button views cover the six directions, and hovering a vessel names it.

3dtry.github.io/braincharter_arterial_venous_connected/braincharter_arterial_venous_connected.html
