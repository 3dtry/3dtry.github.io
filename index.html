Vascular atlas

Three-dimensional visualizations of the heart, neck and brain vasculature. Every scene draws on the SPL Head & Neck Atlas, the JHU parcellation and BrainCharter data; the space is RAS, in mm. The info boxes note that the scan is cut at z ≈ −160 mm and that the JHU alignment is approximate (scale 0.865, rotation 3.6°, median residual 1.2 mm).

Delay investigated in the context of vessels

The blood arrival delays painted onto the vessel trees themselves, at two spatial resolutions.

Arteries and veins coloured by blood arrival delayResolution: JHU atlasdelay_and_vessels↗

The BrainCharter arterial and venous trees painted with the group-average rapidtide delay of the JHU territories: each arterial point takes the delay of the territory it lies in, each vein the mean over the territories it drains. Arteries, veins or both, the delay-territory map as an overlay, hover for vessel names, click to trace a vessel back to its source.

Arteries and veins coloured by voxel-level blood arrival delayResolution: voxel leveldelay_vessels_voxel_resolution↗

The same vessel trees carried to the voxel level: instead of one delay per JHU territory, each arterial and venous point takes the voxel-wise rapidtide delay measured where it lies, so the delay gradient can be followed along the vessels themselves. Arteries, veins or both, in split views.

3dtry.github.io/delay_vessels_voxel_resolution/arteries_veins_voxdelay_split.html
